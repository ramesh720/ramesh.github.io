About Me

I am a PhD Research Scholar at the Indian Institute of Technology, Hyderabad, working on cutting-edge research in speech processing and artificial intelligence. My doctoral thesis focuses on Unified Neural Architectures for Voice Search, exploring innovative approaches to keyword spotting and spoken term detection.

With a strong foundation in electrical engineering and a passion for speech technology, I have contributed to multiple high-impact publications and worked on significant projects including the National Language Translation Mission and Robot Audition systems for DRDO.
